How it works

  1. At the cafeteria, generate a ClienUp QR. The cashier scans it.
  2. After eating, go to the recycling centre and generate the recycling (bin) QR. Staff scans it and the bin opens.
  3. After the bin QR is generated, you can optionally record proof video.
  4. Redeem points for rewards.
